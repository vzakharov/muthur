```
An idle session parked through a relay summary is often the cheaper
and more convenient way back than one kept warm wake after wake, and
operators differ on which they prefer — so the number of renewals is
a setting, and its default is none.

CACHE_KEEPALIVE_WAKES becomes MUTHUR_CACHE_KEEPALIVE_RENEWALS: the
wakes that only keep the prompt cache warm. The wake after the last
renewal parks the session through /relay park. Unset,
the count is 0, so the first wake parks. A negative count or lead is
rejected like a malformed one. The keepalive's other settings take
the same prefix, MUTHUR_CACHE_KEEPALIVE and MUTHUR_CACHE_KEEPALIVE_LEAD,
so a repo running muthur tells them apart from its own.

The keepalive's contract states the cost that bounds the count: a
renewal is two requests reading the whole cached conversation, 10% of
a cold re-cache on the usual multipliers and 5% where the read rate is
1/40 of a one-hour write. Five unused renewals cost 25-50% of the
re-cache they insure against, 10-20 the whole of it, so more than 5 is
not recommended. The cited re-cache ratio is 20-40 times a read, by
model.

Adopters: check whether CACHE_KEEPALIVE, _LEAD or _WAKES is set, in
the session's environment or a .claude/settings*.json env block. Most
trees set none, and then there is nothing to do. One that is set is
read by nothing now: ask your operator to rename it to its MUTHUR_
form, _WAKES becoming _RENEWALS, where they set it.
```
